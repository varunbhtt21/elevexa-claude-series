## 📖 Introduction

Batman is one of the most famous fictional characters in the world. Unlike Superman, he has **no superpowers**. He fights crime with his mind, his body, his money and his determination, and that is a big part of why readers have loved him for more than eighty years.

---

## 🌑 Origin Story

### The Tragedy
When Bruce Wayne was a child, a robber shot his parents, **Thomas and Martha Wayne**, in an alley as the family walked home from the cinema. Bruce watched it happen.

### The Vow
That night, Bruce swore to fight the criminals who had taken his family from him.

### The Training
He spent years traveling the world, studying:
- Martial arts
- Detective work
- Science and criminology

### The Symbol
When he returned to Gotham, he needed a symbol that would frighten criminals. A bat flew through his window, and he took it as a sign. **He became Batman.**

- 🎭 **Disguise:** a master of hiding his identity

## 🏁 Conclusion

Batman endures because **he is human**. He turned personal tragedy into a lifelong mission to protect others.

> 💡 **Key message:** Courage, discipline and intelligence can make an ordinary person a hero.
